SMARTFLOW UI

Figma

The Problem Rapid urbanization in Lipa City has led to worsening traffic in key commercial zones, such as SM City Lipa, Robinsons Place, and the Poblacion area. Current traffic management heavily relies on human enforcers stationed at intersections, a reactive approach that is difficult to scale and prone to delayed responses. Furthermore, there is no centralized, data-driven mechanism to proactively manage vehicle flow or automatically record violations in real time.

The Solution & Concept Designed as a decision-support tool for the City Traffic and Management Office (CTMO), this platform digitally replicates the "pace car" (or rolling roadblock) strategy. By utilizing computer vision from CCTV feeds, the system is conceptualized to calculate optimal speed limits and dynamically display them on electronic Variable Message Signs (VMS) to create a self-regulating traffic stream. My role was to design the centralized web dashboard that makes this complex data actionable for traffic enforcers.

Core Dashboard Features (UI/UX Highlights)

  • Real-Time Monitoring Interface: Features a color-coded map and status view of Lipa City that updates traffic conditions—Free Flow (green), Moderate (yellow), and Congested (red)—in real time based on computed vehicle density.
  • Dynamic Speed Limit Engine:> Displays the actively computed speed limit recommendations (e.g., 20 km/h for congested areas) that are sent to VMS boards across monitored zones.
  • Automated Violation Log: A dedicated, searchable data table that logs vehicles exceeding displayed limits, complete with a timestamp, zone location, estimated speed, and a captured snapshot of the incident.
  • Alerts & Reporting Workflows: Integrates an automatic alert system to notify on-duty personnel of critical congestion levels, alongside tools to generate daily and weekly historical traffic trend reports.
